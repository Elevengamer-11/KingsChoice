Knight Trainig Guide

What type of Knights are there?

  • None Aura Knights (2 Stae Knights)
  • Lover Knights (Knights who get theire Aura from a Lover like Dante and Bice)
  • Aura Item Knights (Arthurians with Shields, Monarchs with Crowns etc)

How do Knights gain Strength?

Basicly the Knights gain there Strength from theire Talents what are shown in Strength (Red), Intellect (Blue), Leadership (Green), Charism (Purple).

    What are the different Talents used for?

  • Strength - Reduces the Amount of Troops consumed in the Outpost
  • Leaderhsip - The Amount of Grain that is collected in the Collections Area
  • Intellect - The Amount of Silver that is collected in the Collections Area
  • Charism - The Amount of Troops that is collected in the Collections Area

How do Takents work?

Talents have a specific amount of stars influencing the chance of converting an Edict into Power or the Amount of Exp required to upgrade the talent. The higher the Talent is the more points you get but also the more edicts you will need to use. BAsicly a 6* talent gives you about 6 times more power then a 1 Star talent but alkso requires about 6 times as much edicts depending on luck with the percentage chances

So what Knights should be Trained?

Basicly there is an Item Aura based Knight that has a High number of Talents for each Type. What does that mean? If you look up on Picking a Knight for f.e. Leadership, you will look at 2 things:

  1. To be suitable to be used for Training a Knight should Require a high count of Talent stars in the Specific Talent you want to build him. To stick with the Leadership example, the Philophers (Book Item) have about 8 Stars Leadership in Total and are a good Option as they are easy to get too
  2. What is also very Important is the Aura of a Knight. The Aura multiplys the Power coming from the Talents. The easiest Aura to get is on the Knights you get for Exchanging Items. Lovers Aura is also a way to increase knight aura but its harder to increase as it Requiers Charm

So if you look to build Knights focus on 1 Knight per Talent as Main Knights. Recommend would be following:

    For all Players

  • Intellct Knight - Monarch (Crowns)
  • Strength Knight - Arthurian (Shields)
  • Leadership Knight - Philospher (Books)
  • Charism Knight - Epic Hero (Swords)

    For Players who want to spend a lot of Money (High Vip levels)

  • All Knights from ViP 9 and above have a crazy Talent-Star count combined with a lover Aura. The Aura is hard to build but the Knights Strength can Reach a incredible Strength from the Talent amount

Summary

  • Use Knights where a high Aura is easy to Reach (Monarch,Arthurians etc) as your Main Knights
  • Focus on 4 Main Knights for each Talent 1 (1 Charism best an Epic Hero, 1 Intellect best a Monarch, 1 Strength best an Arthurian, 1 Leadership best an Philosopher)
  • Use Lover Knights as Backups or those where you have too many Aura Items
  • Save your Edicts due to the percentage convertion when used
  • Build high Star Talents first as they give more power
  • Personal Advice: Use a Intellect Knight to be your first Diamond Knight, it will make it easier to reach the high Silver amount needed to upgrade the next diamond knight
  • Dont forget building the Coopers Store on the Champagne Fair for Charm Items for your Lovers
